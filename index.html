Welcome to the CV Generator

This tool helps you create a professional CV in a few steps:

  • Fill out all your personal and professional details.
  • For sections like Education, Skills, Experience, etc., please use comma-separated values.
  • Example: B.Sc in CSE, M.Sc in CSE
  • Upload your photo and signature.
  • Click "Generate CV" to preview your document.
  • You can download the CV as a PDF once it is generated.
